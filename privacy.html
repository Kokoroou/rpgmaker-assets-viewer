Privacy

Your files never leave your device. MakerLens Assets and MakerLens Events read and decrypt your game in your browser. No file, folder name or key is sent anywhere, and your browser enforces it with a Content-Security-Policy. Once the page has loaded it works offline.

Your files

  • Nothing is uploaded. When you pick a folder, Chrome may say "upload": that is its standard wording for giving a page access to a folder. The files are only read locally.
  • A game link (made inside a game published with rpgmaker-web-toolkit) carries its key after the #, which browsers never send to a server. Your browser sends it only to that game's own site. The files go straight from there into your browser, not through MakerLens.
  • The Assets | Events switch passes the open folder to the other tool inside your browser only. Nothing is sent.
  • The tools only read: your game is never modified.
  • Your theme, sidebar size and whether you answered the feedback card are kept in your browser's localStorage. They never leave your device.

Anonymous usage count

Each page load on the MakerLens site sends one request to GoatCounter: the page path, the referring site and any campaign parameter such as utm_source. A few actions are also counted by name only: the feedback card was shown, skipped or answered, and someone switched between Assets and Events. No cookies or identifiers, no IP address stored. Nothing is sent with Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, or on a local copy. If it is blocked, the tools work the same.

Feedback (optional)

Nothing is sent unless you tap a star or press Send. A message contains the rating, the comment you typed, your browser language, roughly how many games you have opened and, for bug reports only (you can remove it), your browser and system. Never file or folder names, game titles or keys. The message is stored with its date but no IP address or identifier, and is kept until deleted. Please don't put personal information in a comment. To get a reply or have a comment removed, open a GitHub issue.

Hosting and contact

The site is served by Cloudflare Workers (the old address by GitHub Pages). Like any host, they see your IP address to deliver the pages, under their own privacy policies; request logs for the MakerLens Worker are off. MakerLens is a personal open-source project by Trương Tuấn Anh (Kokoroou). The code, including what is sent above, is public on GitHub (Events). For a privacy question or request, open an issue there.
